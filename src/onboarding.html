SinBlocker

Break free from addiction

Keep browsing with unwanted images and videos hidden by default outside trusted sites. Even on untrusted sites, you can navigate and read pages without allowing the whole site's media.

1 No account, no spying, fully local filtering

Free and maintained by eyeofastartes, with no sign-in, browsing telemetry, or external filtering servers. Filtering works entirely on your device.

Blocklists are always one step behind. This starts with media hidden and allows trusted sites instead. That catches new & hidden domains automatically, while built-in exceptions for trusted sites and education domains keep setup simple.

2 Lock it down

Protect your preferences with the in options: choose Password Lock or Challenge Lock to gate settings changes and disable popup controls and the keyboard toggle. Settings also let you enforce SafeSearch independently.

Turn blocking on (already on) → Media hidden outside trusted sites → Lock your settings

When unlocked, use the toolbar popup to allow the current site or press Alt+I to toggle blocking. Locks protect settings, not Chrome's ability to disable or remove the extension.
